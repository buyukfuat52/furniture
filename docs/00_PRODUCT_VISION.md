# Product Vision

MobilyaOS is a vertical B2B platform for custom furniture companies. It joins configure-price-quote, product templates, deterministic manufacturing calculations, supplier pricing, project/revision management, document generation, and controlled agentic operations into one traceable data chain.

## Primary users
- Sales / estimator
- Interior architect / designer
- Costing specialist
- Procurement specialist
- Production planner and workshop operator
- Company administrator
- Management viewer

## Core promise
A quote total must be explainable down to the product revision, part, operation, supplier price observation, formula version, quantity, and approval decision that produced it.

## Product principles
1. Deterministic core, conversational shell.
2. Human approval at financial and external-action boundaries.
3. Revision immutability and reproducibility.
4. Domain-first UX, not a generic CRM or chatbot.
5. One data chain from sales to production.
6. Safe local-first development and provider-neutral AI.
7. Modular monolith first; extract services only when operational evidence demands it.

## Success metrics
- Quote preparation time
- Percentage of quote values with complete provenance
- BOM/production package correction rate
- Sheet utilization and reusable offcut rate
- Revision turnaround time
- Agent tool-call success and human correction rate
- Cross-tenant access violations: zero
